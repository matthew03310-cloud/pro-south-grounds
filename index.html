Everything you need to know about our services.

What services does Pro South Grounds offer?

We provide lawn care, landscaping, clean ups, gutter clean outs, soft washing, and mulch & bed maintenance across Georgia. No job is too big or too small.

Does Pro South Grounds provide free estimates?

Yes! Call 706-881-2136 for a free, no-obligation estimate. We offer flexible scheduling including weekly, bi-weekly, and one-time service.

Is Pro South Grounds licensed and insured?

Yes, Pro South Grounds is a licensed, insured, family-run local business rated 5.0 on Google. You can trust your property is in good hands.

What is soft washing?

Soft washing uses low-pressure cleaning to safely remove algae, mold, mildew, and dirt from siding, decks, driveways, and other surfaces without damaging them.

How often should my lawn be mowed?

Most Georgia lawns benefit from weekly or bi-weekly mowing during the growing season. We'll set up a schedule that keeps your lawn healthy and looking great year round.
